Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains how the SRM Prime, Ramapuram MedFlow gateway handles information. It applies to this gateway page, which links to the Manager's Tab and the Doctor App.

Information this page collects

This gateway page does not ask you to create an account and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It does not collect personal information on its own.

Information in the portals

The Manager's Tab and the Doctor App display and update hospital operational data such as ward capacity, bed status, clinic queues and patient records. This data is handled for hospital operations and patient care only. Access should be limited to authorised hospital managers and on-duty physicians.

Third-party resources

This page loads the Inter typeface from Google Fonts and the three.js library from cdnjs. When your browser requests these files, those services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser details, under their own policies.

Browser storage

The portals may use your browser's local storage to keep the Manager's Tab and the Doctor App in sync across open tabs. This data stays in your browser and can be cleared from your browser settings.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled through MedFlow, but no system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Please do not share your access with unauthorised people.

Your choices

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of information about you by contacting the SRM Prime, Ramapuram administration, subject to medical record retention requirements under applicable law.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows when it was last changed.
